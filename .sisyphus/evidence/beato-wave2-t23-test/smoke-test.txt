## Auth Notes

- Token format: {request_id}:{timestamp}:{operation}:{hmac_signature}
- HMAC data: request_id + timestamp + operation (concatenated, no separators)
- Metadata key: "x-request-token"
- Secret path: /run/armorclaw/secrets/office-hmac (bridge volume)
